# CAMSAI Supporters and Advisory Council

> **Interim draft — pre-incorporation.** This document has not been ratified; a
> version date tag is applied when it is. Last revised 2026-08-20.

This register records who bears the cost of running CAMSAI, and who sits on the
Advisory Council. It exists so that the burden is visible and can be shared,
rather than resting silently on whoever happens to be paying.

**Support confers no governance rights** — no seat, no vote, no veto, and no
priority in any project's roadmap. The terms are in [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md)
§9.4, and they are not negotiable, because what the Consortium is worth to each
of its participants depends on the fact that it cannot be bought.

## Advisory Council

A standing, non-voting forum with a direct channel to the Steering Committee:
two joint meetings a year, the right to place an item on the agenda and have it
answered on the record, advance notice of amendments and project intake, and
the right to file a written position that is recorded with the decision. The
full terms, including what the Council explicitly does not confer, are in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §5.

Admission is by recorded vote of the Steering Committee. There is no fee, and a
diversified organization may participate through more than one division.

## Offering support, or joining the Council

Open an issue in this repository. Say what you would like to contribute and, if
you are seeking a seat on the Advisory Council, which parts of your
organization would take part. The Steering Committee answers on the record.
