## Filling a seat

Interim seats are filled by the founding participants under GOVERNANCE.md §3.
Once the interim period ends, a vacant seat is filled by recorded vote of the
seated members after a public call for candidates open for at least
twenty-eight days.

To express interest in a seat, or to nominate someone, open an issue in this
repository.

## Changes to this roster

Every change to this file is the result of an appointment, resignation, or
removal recorded under GOVERNANCE.md §3. The pull request making the change
must link to that record.
