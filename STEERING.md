The Steering Committee has seven seats, allocated by constituency. The rules
governing composition, the founding period, terms, and vacancy are in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §§1-3.

## Convener

The Convener runs the founding period: maintaining the public call for
candidates, answering expressions of interest on the record, and bringing
appointments to a decision. The role carries **no vote** and ends when a Chair
is elected (GOVERNANCE.md §3.1).

## Expressing interest in a seat

Any individual or organization may propose a candidate, including themselves,
at any time, by **opening an issue in this repository**. During the founding
period the Convener must answer on the record; no expression of interest is
left unanswered. After the founding period, a vacant seat is filled by recorded
vote of the seated members following a public call open for at least
twenty-eight days.

If you are unsure which constituency you belong to, propose the one that fits
best and say why; the Steering Committee would rather resolve that in the open
than have a candidate self-exclude.
