# CAMSAI Name, Marks, and Domain

> **Interim draft — pre-incorporation.** Version `2026.8.20-0`, 20 August 2026.

## 1. What this covers

- The names **CAMSAI** and **Consortium for the Advancement of Materials
  Science with AI**.
- The CAMSAI logo and other visual identifiers, as adopted.
- The domain **camsai.org** and the GitHub organization **camsai**.

Together, "the CAMSAI marks".

## 2. Interim custody

CAMSAI is not incorporated and cannot hold property in its own name
([CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) §8). The CAMSAI marks are therefore held in interim
custody by **Mat3ra**, which owns the camsai.org domain and administers the
GitHub organization.

The custodian holds the marks **for the benefit of the Consortium**, not for
its own benefit, and undertakes:

1. **To assign.** To transfer the marks, the domain, and administrative
   control of the GitHub organization to the Consortium's permanent home — its
   fiscal sponsor or a dedicated legal entity — once one is established under
   Charter §8, on the Steering Committee's request and without additional
   consideration.
2. **Not to encumber.** Not to sell, license exclusively, pledge, or otherwise
   encumber the marks, and not to use them to assert control over the direction
   of the Consortium or any of its projects.
3. **To act on direction.** To exercise custody in accordance with recorded
   decisions of the Steering Committee, including decisions about permitted
   use of the marks.
4. **To maintain.** To keep the domain registration and any registrations
   current for the duration of custody.

This section records the terms of custody in the open so that participants and
their institutions can see them. It is not itself a transfer of rights; a
definitive assignment will be executed when there is an entity to receive it.

## 3. Copyright notices in projects

Copyright notices in CAMSAI project repositories reading "CAMSAI.org and
contributors" are collective attribution to the body of contributors, in the
customary open-source form. They do not assert that an entity holds those
rights. Copyright in a contribution remains with its author or their employer,
licensed to the public under the project's outbound license.

## 4. Permitted use

Without seeking permission, anyone may:

- state truthfully that software is a CAMSAI project, is built on one, or is
  compatible with one;
- refer to CAMSAI and its projects by name in articles, talks, papers,
  courses, and documentation;
- redistribute unmodified CAMSAI releases under their existing licenses,
  keeping their names and notices intact.

Permission is required to:

- use the CAMSAI marks in the name or logo of a product, service, company,
  event, or organization;
- use them in a way that suggests CAMSAI endorses, sponsors, certifies, or is
  affiliated with something it has not agreed to;
- apply the CAMSAI name to a modified build of a CAMSAI project distributed to
  others. Modified builds must be renamed. The underlying open-source license
  permits the modification and its distribution; it does not grant use of the
  name.

To request permission, open an issue in this repository. Requests are decided
by the Steering Committee under GOVERNANCE.md §4.

## 5. Projects hosted by CAMSAI

Admission of a project to CAMSAI is a decision about stewardship, not a
transfer of ownership (Charter §5). A project that leaves CAMSAI, or is
archived, keeps its code under its existing license but ceases to use the
CAMSAI marks in its name and branding.
